# Getting Started

Welcome. You do not need to finish a huge lesson every day. Aim for one focused
session of 30-90 minutes and leave a small trail of evidence: notes, commands,
configuration, a screenshot, or a short reflection.

## Start Day 001

1. Open [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and read the Day 001 row.
2. Open [your Day 001 log](days/day-001-devops-mindset/README.md).
3. Complete the delivery-flow exercise in that file.
4. Replace the reflection placeholder with what you learned.
5. Use [the Git guide](GIT_GUIDE.md) to push your work to GitHub.

## Start a Later Day

Open the [daily worksheet index](days/README.md), select the current day, and
follow its research prompts and hands-on steps. Each worksheet tells you what
evidence to add before updating [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) and publishing your
changes.

## Add Practical Work

The roadmap tells you what to learn each day. The [labs](labs/README.md) give
you guided exercises when you want more practice. The [projects](projects/README.md)
are larger pieces to build gradually at the end of each 20-day phase.
